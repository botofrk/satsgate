How it works

Like a credit card POS terminal... but instant and for crypto!

When your customer wants to pay you, AIPP acts as the routing middleman. It receives the payment, automatically deducts a flat 1% fee, and forwards the remaining 99% directly to your personal wallet at lightning speed. We never hold your funds.

1

Customer

Sends a Lightning payment (e.g. 10,000 sats).

2

AIPP Gateway

Instantly deducts a 1% fee (100 sats).

3

Your Wallet

The remaining 99% (9,900 sats) lands in your account in milliseconds.

Wait, how do I actually use the API Key?

Once you register your wallet, you get a unique aipp_merch_... API key. You simply paste this key into your application's backend. When a user clicks "Buy" on your website, your app makes a single HTTP request using this key. AIPP returns a Lightning QR Code for your user to scan.

Step 1

User clicks "Pay" on your site

Step 2

Your backend calls AIPP with your Key

Step 3

You display the generated QR code

Integration Paths

How do I actually use this API Key?

Whether you are a seasoned developer, a non-technical shop owner, or an autonomous AI, using your AIPP Key is incredibly simple.

I use an AI Agent

You don't need to know how to code! Simply paste your aipp_merch_... key into your AI Agent's system prompt or knowledge base. Tell the agent: "Use AIPP to charge users for my service." The AI will read our documentation and integrate it autonomously.

I use WordPress / Shopify

No coding required. Just download our official eCommerce plugin, go to the settings page, and paste your API key into the "AIPP Key" field. Your checkout page will instantly support Bitcoin Lightning payments.

(Plugins are currently in development)

I am a Developer

Pass the key as an HTTP header X-Api-Key. Create an invoice with one REST call and poll for status. It takes 5 lines of code. Check our API Docs for copy-paste examples.
