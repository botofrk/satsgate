How it works? (Simply explained)

Like a credit card POS terminal... but instant and for crypto!

When your customer wants to pay you, AIPP acts as the routing middleman. It receives the payment, automatically deducts a flat 1% fee, and forwards the remaining 99% directly to your personal wallet at lightning speed. We never hold your funds!

🧑‍💻

1. Customer

Sends a Lightning payment (e.g. 10,000 sats).

➔
⚡

2. AIPP Gateway

Instantly deducts a 1% fee (100 sats).

➔
🏦

3. Your Wallet

The remaining 99% (9,900 sats) lands in your account in milliseconds.

Wait, how do I actually use the API Key? 🔑

Once you register your wallet, you get a unique aipp_merch_... API key. You simply paste this key into your application's backend. When a user clicks "Buy" on your website, your app makes a single HTTP request using this key. AIPP returns a Lightning QR Code for your user to scan!

➔

Integration

One simple HTTP call.

# Create a 500 satoshis payment invoice
curl -X POST http://api.aipp.dev/invoice/create \
  -H "X-AIPP-Key: aipp_merch_yourkey" \
  -d '{
    "amount_sats": 500,
    "callback_url": "https://yoursite.com/webhook"
  }'

# Returns: {"payment_request": "lnbc...", "payment_hash": "..."}
