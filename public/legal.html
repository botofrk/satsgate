aipp — Open-source non-custodial Smart Tag publishing software

Last Updated: August 2026

01 Non-Custodial Software Status

AIPP developers do not provide banking, custody, money transmission, or exchange services.

aipp is an open-source, non-custodial Smart Tag publishing software. It allows creators and developers to attach a cryptographic price tag to any digital asset, link, or content, and collect settlement directly into their own self-custody wallets.

  • The software mints Smart Tags that route settlements directly to destinations specified by the creator (Lightning Address or EVM wallet address).
  • Developers have no access to, control over, or possession of users' private keys, funds, or databases.
  • aipp does not hold any funds in transit — all settlements are direct wallet-to-wallet transfers initiated by the buyer's own wallet.

02 Nature of the 1% Platform Fee

The flat 1% routing fee applied by AIPP.dev is not a financial commission or money transmission service fee, and is subject to a minimum floor of 5 satoshis per transaction.

  • This fee is a "Decentralized Software Routing & Licensing Fee" designed to support the ongoing development, updates, and maintenance of the open-source software and its ecosystem.
  • Paralleling standard practices in Web3 and decentralized finance (DeFi) — such as MetaMask Swap fees or Uniswap router fees — this split is executed autonomously at the software code level.
  • Funds are never pooled, held, or custodied by the developers before distribution.

04 Privacy Policy

AIPP.dev is designed from the ground up to respect user and merchant privacy:

  • Minimal Data & Zero Tracking: We collect only the data strictly necessary to operate the Smart Tag software — your Lightning Address and/or Base wallet address (required for direct settlement). We do not collect names, national IDs, browsing history, or personal identifiers. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.
  • Chrome Web Store Limited Use Policy: The use of information received from Google APIs by the AIPP Chrome Extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. User data is never sold, transferred to third parties, or used for credit scoring or advertising.
  • No Cryptocurrency Mining: The AIPP browser extension does not engage in cryptocurrency mining, background distributed computing, or unauthorized hardware utilization. It operates solely as a user-initiated intent handler and micropayment checkout tool.
  • Local Databases & Retention: All transactional records are stored in a private database on the self-hosted node. Inactive accounts with zero balance are automatically purged after 30 days.
