Last Updated: July 15, 2026

AIPP.dev is an open-source, non-custodial payment routing software designed to be deployed and operated by merchants and AI agents on their own self-hosted infrastructure. This document outlines the legal status of the software, terms of use, and the nature of the platform fees.

1. Non-Custodial Software Status

AIPP.dev developers and contributors do not provide banking, custody, money transmission, or exchange services.

  • The software is designed to instantly route payments directly to destinations specified by the user (Lightning Address or EVM wallet addresses).
  • Developers have no access to, control over, or possession of users' private keys, funds, or databases.

2. Nature of the 1% Platform Fee

The flat 1% routing fee applied by AIPP.dev is not a financial commission or money transmission service fee.

  • This fee is a "Decentralized Software Routing & Licensing Fee" designed to support the ongoing development, updates, and maintenance of the open-source software and its ecosystem.
  • Paralleling standard practices in Web3 and decentralized finance (DeFi) (such as MetaMask Swap fees or Uniswap router fees), this split is executed autonomously at the software code level. Funds are never pooled, held, or custodied by the developers before distribution.

4. Privacy Policy

AIPP.dev is designed from the ground up to respect user and merchant privacy:

  • Minimal Data: We collect only the data strictly necessary to operate the payment gateway: your Lightning Address and/or Base wallet address (required for payouts), and an optional email address for transactional notifications. We do not collect names, national IDs, or any other personal identifiers.
  • No Tracking: We do not use cookies, tracking scripts, or analytics tools.
  • Local Databases: All transactional records (invoice logs, balances) are stored in a private SQLite database on the gateway server. This data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for marketing purposes.
  • Data Deletion: Inactive merchant accounts with no balance are automatically purged after 30 days of inactivity. You may also request manual deletion by contacting support.
