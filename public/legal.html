01 Software and Settlement Model

AIPP is Smart Tag and payment-automation software. Legal classification depends on the deployment and jurisdiction.

AIPP allows creators and developers to attach a price tag to digital assets, links and content. In the hosted Lightning flow, buyer funds may reach AIPP before the merchant amount is forwarded.

  • Merchants specify a Lightning Address or EVM payout destination.
  • AIPP does not require merchant wallet private keys.
  • Hosted payment flows may receive funds before automatically forwarding merchant proceeds. AIPP does not provide a general-purpose merchant spending balance.

02 Nature of the 1% Platform Fee

For Lightning, the buyer currently pays the merchant price plus ceil(1%) + 5 satoshis. This is an AIPP platform fee; it is not described as a network fee.

  • The fee supports operation and development of the hosted software.
  • The checkout should show the merchant price, AIPP fee and buyer total separately.
  • This page makes no legal conclusion about custody, money transmission, tax or licensing status.

04 Privacy Policy

AIPP.dev is designed from the ground up to respect user and merchant privacy:

  • Minimal Data Collection: We collect only the data strictly necessary to operate the Smart Tag software — your Lightning Address and/or Base wallet address (required for automated settlement). We do not collect names, national IDs, browsing history, or personal identifiers. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.
  • Chrome Web Store Limited Use Policy: The use of information received from Google APIs by the AIPP Chrome Extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. User data is never sold, transferred to third parties, or used for credit scoring or advertising.
  • No Cryptocurrency Mining: The AIPP browser extension does not engage in cryptocurrency mining, background distributed computing, or unauthorized hardware utilization. It operates solely as a user-initiated intent handler and micropayment checkout tool.
  • Local Databases & Retention: All transactional records are stored in a private database on the self-hosted node. Inactive accounts with zero balance are automatically purged after 30 days.
